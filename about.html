About Filtered

We believe every parent deserves peace of mind, even when co-parenting is difficult.

Our Mission

Filtered exists for one reason: to help parents stay connected to important information about their children without suffering the emotional toll of hostile communication.

We know you can't just block your co-parent. You need to coordinate pickups, discuss schedules, and handle emergencies. But that doesn't mean you have to absorb their anger, manipulation, or emotional abuse every time your phone buzzes.

"You need to co-parent. You don't need to absorb their anger."

Filtered uses AI to read the hostility so you don't have to. You see the information that matters—the pickup time, the schedule change, the question about the school event—without the attacks, guilt trips, or manipulation wrapped around it.

Why We Built This

Filtered was born from lived experience. We've seen firsthand how a single hostile text can derail an entire day. How the anxiety of checking your phone becomes a constant weight. How difficult it is to be a present, patient parent when you're still reeling from your co-parent's latest attack.

Existing solutions weren't enough. Blocking isn't an option when you need to co-parent. Apps that just log messages don't protect your mental health in the moment. And therapists can only help you process after the damage is done.

We built Filtered to intervene in real-time—to give you the shield you need before the words hit you, while still keeping you informed and connected as a parent.

Our Values

Protection First

Your mental health comes first. We filter aggressively by default because we'd rather over-protect than under-protect.

You're in Control

See originals when you choose. Turn filtering on or off. Export for legal needs. It's your data, your choice.

Privacy Always

Your messages are encrypted and private. We never sell your data. No human reads your messages—only AI.

Children First

Everything we do serves one goal: helping you be a better, more present parent for your children.
