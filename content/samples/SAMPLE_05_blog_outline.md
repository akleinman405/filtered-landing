## BLOG OUTLINE:

### Introduction (150 words)
- Hook: The moment you see their name pop up and your stomach drops
- Validate the experience (you're not alone, this is hard)
- Promise: Practical scripts and strategies that actually work
- Brief mention of what we'll cover

---

### Section 1: Why Co-Parent Texts Feel So Triggering (250 words)
- Brief nervous system explanation (not pseudo-science)
- Fight-or-flight response to perceived threats
- Why your body reacts before your brain catches up
- Normalize the experience
- Transition: Understanding this helps you respond, not react

---

### Section 2: The BIFF Method Explained (300 words)
- What BIFF stands for (Brief, Informative, Friendly, Firm)
- Origin: Bill Eddy, High Conflict Institute
- Why it works for high-conflict communication
- When to use it (and when other approaches might be better)

**Subheading: BIFF in Practice**
- Brief: One paragraph max, often one sentence
- Informative: Just the facts, no emotions or opinions
- Friendly: Neutral tone, not aggressive OR overly warm
- Firm: Ends the conversation, doesn't invite debate

---

### Section 3: 5 Common Scenarios + Scripts (500 words)

**Scenario 1: The Accusation Avalanche**
- What it looks like: Long message listing everything you've done wrong
- Your response: Acknowledge + redirect to action item
- Example script: "Thanks for sharing your concerns. To confirm the schedule: [specific detail]. Let me know if anything changes."

**Scenario 2: The Guilt Trip**
- What it looks like: "The kids were so sad you didn't..."
- Your response: Don't take the bait, stick to facts
- Example script: "I understand. [Child] and I had a good time together. Looking forward to next weekend."

**Scenario 3: The Demand for Immediate Response**
- What it looks like: "I need an answer NOW"
- Your response: Establish your timeline without defensiveness
- Example script: "I'll review this and respond by [reasonable timeframe]."

**Scenario 4: Bringing Up the Past**
- What it looks like: "Just like when you..."
- Your response: Stay present-focused
- Example script: "I'm focused on [current issue]. What do you need for [specific current thing]?"

**Scenario 5: The Passive-Aggressive Comment**
- What it looks like: "Must be nice to..."
- Your response: Ignore the jab, respond to substance (if any)
- Example script: [Only respond if there's an actual question/request. Otherwise, no response needed.]

---

### Section 4: When NOT to Respond (200 words)
- Not every message deserves a reply
- Questions to ask: Is there an action item? Is there a deadline? Will my kids be affected?
- The power of strategic silence
- Difference between ignoring and choosing not to engage

---

### Section 5: Protecting Your Peace Long-Term (200 words)

- Building your support system
- When to consider parallel parenting
