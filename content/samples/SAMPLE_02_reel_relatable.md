# Sample Post #2: Instagram Reel
## Type: Relatable Moment (with hopeful ending)

**Post Type:** Reel (30-45 seconds)
**Platform:** Instagram / TikTok
**Content Pillar:** Emotional Validation (25%)

---

### VIDEO SCRIPT:

**[0-3 sec] HOOK**
*On screen text:* "The co-parent communication evolution"
*Person looking stressed at phone*

**[3-8 sec] PAST - SCENE 1**
*Text overlay:* "Year 1"
*Action:* Person typing furiously, deleting, retyping, throwing hands up
*Expression:* Frustrated, anxious

**[8-13 sec] PAST - SCENE 2**
*Text overlay:* "Drafting text #47"
*Action:* Person stress-pacing, looking at phone, drafting another response
*Expression:* Overwhelmed

**[13-18 sec] TRANSITION**
*Text overlay:* "Then I learned..."
*Action:* Deep breath, setting phone down
*Expression:* Contemplative

**[18-23 sec] PRESENT - SCENE 1**
*Text overlay:* "Now"
*Action:* Reads message calmly, thinks for a moment
*Expression:* Neutral, grounded

**[23-28 sec] PRESENT - SCENE 2**
*Text overlay:* "One sentence. Done."
*Action:* Types brief response, puts phone away, smiles slightly
*Expression:* Relief, peace

**[28-33 sec] END**
*Text overlay:* "The pause button changed everything"
*Final text:* "@filteredapp"
*Action:* Person going about their day peacefully

---

### CAPTION:

```
Year 1: Drafting novel-length responses at 2am
Now: One sentence and done ✓

The difference isn't that they changed.
The difference is I stopped trying to be understood by someone who doesn't want to understand.

Brief. Informative. Friendly. Firm.
That's it. That's the whole strategy.

If you're still in the drafting-47-texts phase, I see you. It gets better.

What was your turning point? 👇
```

### PRODUCTION NOTES:
- Film in natural lighting
- Casual setting (home environment)
- Authentic expressions, not performative
- Background music: Calm, building to hopeful
- Text overlays should be readable without sound
- End on positive note (mandatory per strategy)
