# Sample Post #1: Instagram Carousel
## Series: "Protecting Your Peace: Response Scripts"

**Post Type:** Carousel (7 slides)
**Platform:** Instagram
**Content Pillar:** Communication Tools (35%)

---

### SLIDE 1 (Hook)
**Visual:** Clean teal gradient background, bold text
**Text:**
```
When they send a
3-paragraph accusation

↓ Swipe for your response ↓
```

---

### SLIDE 2 (The Scenario)
**Visual:** White background, soft gray text box
**Text:**
```
The message:

A wall of text about everything
you've "done wrong" this month,
last year, and possibly in a
past life.

Your stomach drops.
Your fingers start typing a defense.

Wait.
```

---

### SLIDE 3 (The Pause)
**Visual:** Calming blue/teal gradient
**Text:**
```
Before you respond, remember:

You don't have to address
every accusation.

You don't have to defend
your entire character.

You only have to respond
to what matters for your kids.
```

---

### SLIDE 4 (The Response)
**Visual:** White background, response in a "message bubble" style
**Text:**
```
Your response:

"Thanks for sharing your concerns.
To confirm: pickup is still Saturday
at 3pm. Let me know if that changes."

That's it.
```

---

### SLIDE 5 (Why It Works)
**Visual:** Soft background, bullet points
**Text:**
```
Why this works:

✓ Acknowledges without agreeing
✓ Redirects to action items
✓ Doesn't take the bait
✓ Creates documentation of cooperation
✓ Protects your peace
```

---

### SLIDE 6 (The BIFF Check)
**Visual:** Four quadrants with icons
**Text:**
```
BIFF Check ✓

Brief - One sentence
Informative - Confirms the schedule
Friendly - "Thanks for sharing"
Firm - Ends the conversation
```

Three paragraphs of accusations don't require three paragraphs back.

Your job isn't to correct their version of events. Your job is to:
• Protect your peace
• Confirm what matters for your kids
• Document your cooperation

The BIFF method (Brief, Informative, Friendly, Firm) isn't about being a pushover. It's about being strategic with your energy.
