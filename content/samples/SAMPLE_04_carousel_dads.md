# Sample Post #4: Instagram Carousel
## Series: "For All Parents" - Dad-Specific Content

**Post Type:** Carousel (6 slides)
**Platform:** Instagram
**Content Pillar:** Inclusive Content

---

### SLIDE 1 (Hook)
**Visual:** Clean design, bold text
**Text:**
```
Co-parenting as a dad:

5 things you don't
have to explain
```

---

### SLIDE 2
**Visual:** White background, numbered point
**Text:**
```
1. Why you want 50/50

You don't need to justify wanting
to be present in your kids' lives.

Equal involvement isn't "taking"
anything from anyone.
It's parenting.
```

---

### SLIDE 3
**Visual:** White background, numbered point
**Text:**
```
2. Your parenting style

Different doesn't mean wrong.

Your household, your routines.
Consistency within YOUR home
is what matters.
```

---

### SLIDE 4
**Visual:** White background, numbered point
**Text:**
```
3. Your emotions

Dads get triggered too.
Dads feel anxiety before exchanges too.
Dads lose sleep over co-parent texts too.

That's not weakness. That's caring.
```

---

### SLIDE 5
**Visual:** White background, numbered point
**Text:**
```
4. Your boundaries

"I'll respond to that tomorrow"
is a complete sentence.

You don't owe instant replies
to non-emergencies.

5. Your need for support

Asking for help, using tools,
finding your community -
that's strength, not defeat.
```

---

### SLIDE 6 (CTA)
**Visual:** Branded, warm
**Text:**
```
To every dad navigating this:

Your presence matters.
Your peace matters.
You're not alone.

Save this for a hard day.

#coparentingdads #divorceddads
#coparenting #fatherhood
```

### CAPTION:

```
To the dads in the comments: this one's for you.

Co-parenting resources often assume the audience is exclusively moms. But dads are navigating the same anxiety, the same difficult texts, the same sleepless nights wondering if they said the right thing.

You don't have to explain:
• Why you want to be involved
• Why your parenting looks different
• Why this is hard for you too
• Why you need boundaries
• Why you need support

Different perspectives on parenting can coexist. Your kids benefit from knowing both their parents, both their homes, both their ways of doing things.

To the moms reading this: share it with a co-parent dad who might need it.

What would you add to this list? 👇

📱 Filtered works for all co-parents. Link in bio.

#coparenting #coparentingdads #fatherhood #divorceddads #singleparents #peacefulcoparenting
```

### DESIGN NOTES:
- Gender-neutral colors (not blue/stereotypical)
