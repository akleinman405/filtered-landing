## Content Standards Established

### Brand Voice
- "We" voice (not "I/me")
- Validates before educating
- Ends every post on hope/empowerment
- Professional warmth without over-familiarity

### Required Elements
- Educational disclaimers on all advice content
- BIFF credited to Bill Eddy / High Conflict Institute
- Safety disclaimers where applicable
- Soft CTAs ("Link in bio to learn more")

### Prohibited Content
- No hostile message screenshots in demos
- No toxic hashtags (#narcissistabuse, #toxiccoparent)
- No diagnosing co-parents ("narcissist")
- No uncited research claims
- No litigation-focused language

### Production Standards
- All Reels: 9:16 aspect ratio, 30-40 seconds
- Text safe zone: 15% margin from edges
- Text overlay: Bold sans-serif, minimum 48pt
- Captions: Reels ~150 words, Carousels ~150-200 words
