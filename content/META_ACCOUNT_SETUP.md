## Content Scheduling

### Recommended Posting Schedule

Based on the 3-month content calendar:

| Platform | Frequency | Best Times |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| **Instagram Feed** | 3x/week | Tue/Thu/Sat 9-11am or 7-9pm |
| **Instagram Stories** | 5x/week | Mon-Fri, varied times |
| **Facebook** | 2x/week | Wed/Sun 1-3pm |

### Scheduling Tools

**Option 1: Meta Business Suite (Free)**
- Built into Facebook/Instagram
- Schedule up to 75 days ahead
- Basic analytics

**Option 2: Later (Recommended for Instagram-first)**
- Free tier: 30 posts/month per profile
- Paid: $25/month for more features
- Visual calendar, drag-and-drop
- Link in bio management
- Best for planning carousels

**Option 3: Buffer**
- Free tier: 10 posts per channel
- Paid: $6/month/channel
- Clean interface
- Good for simple scheduling

**Option 4: Hootsuite**
- Free tier: 5 posts at once
- Paid: $99/month
- More robust for larger operations

### How to Schedule in Meta Business Suite

1. Go to business.facebook.com
2. Click "Content" → "Planner"
3. Click "Create Post" or "Create Reel"
4. Select accounts (Instagram, Facebook, or both)
5. Add content, caption, first comment (for hashtags)
6. Click clock icon to schedule
7. Select date and time
8. Click "Schedule"

### First Comment Strategy (Instagram)

Schedule your hashtags as a first comment rather than in the caption:
- Keeps caption clean
- Same algorithmic benefit
- Meta Business Suite supports this natively

## Brand Assets Needed

| Facebook | 170x170 px | PNG or JPG |

| Device | Size |
|--------|------|

**Safe zone:** Keep important elements in the center 640x312 area
