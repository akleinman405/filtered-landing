## NOTES FOR FINAL REVIEW

**Before approving any post, ask:**
1. Does this validate WITHOUT creating an echo chamber?
2. Could this be used against a user in court?
3. Does this frame the dynamic, not diagnose the person?
4. Would someone in genuine distress feel helped, not triggered?
5. Is the CTA soft enough for this sensitive space?

- Generic affirmation posts (oversaturated)
- Risky humor content (could mock pain)
- "What to Say Instead" framing (reframed)
- Toxic hashtags (#narcissistabuse, etc.)
- Venting/unmoderated community spaces
