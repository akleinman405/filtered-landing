## CRITICAL ISSUES (All 3 Agents Agreed)

### 1. Website Has Fake Rating Data
**Issue:** Landing page JSON-LD shows 4.8 stars with 150 reviews - app isn't launched
**Risk:** Google penalty, FTC issues, trust destruction
**Action:** REMOVE IMMEDIATELY

### 2. "What to Say Instead" Framing is Wrong
**Issue:** Implies user is communicating wrong, feels preachy
**Fix:** Reframe as "When They Say X, Try Y" or "Protecting Your Peace: Response Scripts"

### 3. Hashtags Could Attract Wrong Audience
**Issue:** #toxiccoparent #narcissistabuse creates echo chamber, validates hostility
**Fix:** REMOVE these entirely. Use neutral: #coparentingcommunication #peacefulcoparenting

### 4. Missing Disclaimers
**Issue:** Providing communication advice that affects custody without legal disclaimer
**Fix:** Add to every post: "Educational content, not legal advice. Consult your attorney."

### 5. Posting Frequency Unsustainable
**Issue:** 16-18 posts/week is burnout recipe for startup
**Fix:** Reduce to 8-10/week: 3 Instagram, 3 TikTok, 2 Facebook, 2 blogs/month

### 6. Generic Quote Posts Are Weak
**Issue:** Affirmation/validation quotes are oversaturated, won't differentiate
**Fix:** Cut static quote posts by 50%, invest in Reels instead

## BRAND VOICE REFINEMENTS

### Tone Check Questions (Before Every Post):
1. Does this blame the user for communicating wrong?
2. Could this be used against a user in court?
3. Does this validate one parent over another?
4. Would someone in genuine distress feel helped or triggered?
