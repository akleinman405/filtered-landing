Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Filtered.

How does the AI filtering work?

Our AI analyzes incoming messages and removes hostile, manipulative, or emotionally charged language while preserving the essential information. You always have the option to view the original message if needed.

Will my co-parent know I'm using Filtered?

Both you and your co-parent use the Filtered app. They'll know you're communicating through Filtered, but the filtering happens only on your end - they send normal messages and won't see which ones triggered filtering.

Can I use the original messages in court?

Yes. All original messages are stored and accessible. You can view, export, and use them for legal documentation. We retain messages for 2 years specifically for this purpose.

What if there's an emergency?

Our AI detects urgent and emergency messages and alerts you immediately, even if the message contains hostile language. You'll never miss something important about your children's safety.

How is this different from blocking them?

Blocking cuts off communication entirely. Filtered keeps you connected and informed while protecting your mental health. You stay in the loop without the emotional toll.

Can I turn off filtering?

Yes. You have full control. You can view individual original messages, or turn on "Show Original Messages" in settings to see everything unfiltered. It's your choice.

What happens after the free trial?

After 14 days, your subscription continues at $9/month. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends and won't be charged. No commitments, no contracts.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All messages are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
