      Frequently Asked Questions

      Everything you need to know about Filtered.

      How does the AI filtering work?

      Our AI analyzes incoming messages and removes hostile, manipulative, or emotionally charged language while preserving the essential information. You always have the option to view the original message if needed.

      Will the other person know I'm using Filtered?

      Both of you use the Filtered app. They'll know you're communicating through Filtered, but the filtering happens only on your end - they send normal messages and won't see which ones triggered filtering.

      Can I use the original messages in court?

      Yes. All original messages are stored and accessible. You can view, export, and use them for legal documentation. We retain messages for 2 years specifically for this purpose.

      What if there's an emergency?

      Our AI detects urgent and emergency messages and alerts you immediately, even if the message contains hostile language. You'll never miss something important.

      How is this different from blocking them?

      Blocking cuts off communication entirely. Filtered keeps you connected and informed while protecting your mental health. You stay in the loop without the emotional toll.

      Can I turn off filtering?

      Absolutely—you're always in control. You can view the original message anytime with a single tap, or turn off filtering for an entire conversation using the toggle at the top of the chat. Many users find the mood indicators and summaries help them decide which originals are worth reading and which they can skip entirely.

      What happens after the free trial?

      After 14 days, your subscription continues at $14.99/month (or $119.88/year to save 33%). You can cancel anytime before the trial ends and won't be charged. No commitments, no contracts.

      Is my data secure?

      Absolutely. All messages are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
