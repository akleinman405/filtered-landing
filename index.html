SMS Messaging Terms

Opt-In Process

When you sign up for Filtered, you explicitly consent to receive SMS messages by:

  • Creating an account and agreeing to our Terms of Service
  • Completing the onboarding process and accepting a dedicated phone number
  • Providing your co-parent with your Filtered number for communication

Message Types

You may receive the following types of SMS messages:

  • Forwarded messages from your co-parent (filtered for hostile content)
  • Emergency alerts for time-sensitive matters
  • Service notifications about your account

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your co-parent communication. You control notification settings within the app.

Opt-Out

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by:

  • Texting STOP to your Filtered number
  • Disabling SMS notifications in app settings
  • Contacting us at alec@kleinmanventures.com

Costs

Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier plan. Filtered does not charge per-message fees to users.

Help

For help, text HELP to your Filtered number or contact alec@kleinmanventures.com

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 29, 2025

Information We Collect

Filtered collects the following information to provide our service:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and authentication credentials
  • Messages: SMS messages sent to and from your Filtered number are encrypted and stored to enable our filtering service
  • Usage Data: App usage patterns to improve our service
  • Device Information: Device type and push notification tokens for alerts

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide AI-powered message filtering and analysis
  • Deliver SMS messages between you and your co-parent
  • Send notifications about important messages
  • Improve our filtering algorithms and service quality
  • Communicate with you about your account

Data Security

All messages are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data. Messages are stored on secure servers and access is strictly limited.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Twilio (SMS delivery), cloud hosting providers
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights and safety

Data Retention

Messages are retained for 2 years to support potential legal documentation needs common in co-parenting situations. You may request data deletion by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at: alec@kleinmanventures.com
