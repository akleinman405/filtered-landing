SMS Messaging Terms

Opt-In Process

When you sign up for Filtered, you explicitly consent to receive SMS messages by:

  • Creating an account and agreeing to our Terms of Service
  • Completing the onboarding process and accepting a dedicated phone number
  • Providing your co-parent with your Filtered number for communication

Message Types

You may receive the following types of SMS messages:

  • Forwarded messages from your co-parent (filtered for hostile content)
  • Emergency alerts for time-sensitive matters
  • Service notifications about your account

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your co-parent communication. You control notification settings within the app.

Opt-Out

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by:

  • Texting STOP to your Filtered number
  • Disabling SMS notifications in app settings
  • Contacting us at alec@kleinmanventures.com

Costs

Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier plan. Filtered does not charge per-message fees to users.

Help

For help, text HELP to your Filtered number or contact alec@kleinmanventures.com
