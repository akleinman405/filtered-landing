# Social Media Marketing Research Plan for Filtered

**Date:** 2026-01-20
**Purpose:** Comprehensive research guide for building a social media marketing campaign for Filtered, an AI-powered co-parent communication app.

---

## Executive Summary

Filtered helps co-parents communicate without the emotional toll of hostile messages. The target audience is separated/divorced parents dealing with high-conflict co-parenting situations. This is a sensitive, emotional topic requiring a thoughtful, empathetic marketing approach.

**Key Challenges:**
- Sensitive subject matter (divorce, conflict, emotional abuse)
- Target audience is often stressed and skeptical
- Need to build trust before asking for conversion
- Must avoid appearing to "take sides" in co-parenting disputes

---

## PART 1: Research Topics for Agents

### 1.1 Industry & Competitor Research

**Research Questions:**
- What social media strategies do competing co-parenting apps use? (OurFamilyWizard, TalkingParents, Cozi, AppClose)
- What content performs best in the "parenting after divorce" niche?
- Which influencers/accounts have large followings in this space?
- What hashtags are most used/searched in co-parenting content?
- What paid advertising strategies work for mental health/wellness apps?

**Sources to Investigate:**
- Competitor social media accounts (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn)
- Social media case studies for wellness/mental health apps (Calm, Headspace, BetterHelp)
- Marketing blogs: HubSpot, Buffer, Hootsuite, Sprout Social
- App marketing resources: AppAnnie, Sensor Tower blogs
- Reddit communities: r/coparenting, r/divorce, r/SingleParents

---

### 1.2 Platform-Specific Best Practices

**For Each Platform, Research:**

#### Instagram
- Optimal posting frequency and times
- Best content formats (Reels vs. Stories vs. Carousels vs. Static posts)
- Hashtag strategy (how many, which ones, branded vs. discovery)
- Bio optimization for app downloads
- Instagram Shopping/Link in Bio tools
- Collaboration/partnership features
- Success metrics and benchmarks for app accounts

#### TikTok
- Algorithm factors and how to get on For You Page
- Content formats that work for educational/emotional content
- Optimal video length for engagement
- Sound/music trends and how to use them appropriately
- Hashtag strategy differences from Instagram
- Creator partnerships and how to find them
- TikTok Ads setup and targeting options

#### Facebook
- Facebook Groups strategy (join existing vs. create own)
- Facebook Page best practices
- Facebook Ads for app installs - targeting options
- Lookalike audiences and custom audiences
- Content that performs well for 30-50 age demographic
- Community management best practices

#### LinkedIn
- B2B angle: marketing to family lawyers, therapists, mediators
- Thought leadership content strategy
- LinkedIn articles vs. posts
- Professional networking in family law space

#### YouTube
- Long-form educational content strategy
- YouTube Shorts for discovery
- SEO for YouTube (titles, descriptions, tags)
- Collaboration with family/divorce content creators

#### Pinterest
- Pin strategy for parenting/self-help content
- Pinterest SEO and keyword research
- Driving traffic from Pinterest to landing page

### 1.3 Content Strategy Research

**Content Pillars to Research:**

1. **Educational Content**
   - What co-parenting communication tips resonate most?
   - What questions do co-parents frequently ask online?
   - What myths about co-parenting need debunking?
   - What legal/practical information do people need?

2. **Emotional/Supportive Content**
   - What affirmations/encouragement work for this audience?
   - How do successful accounts handle sensitive topics?
   - What storytelling approaches build connection?
   - How to share user stories ethically (with permission)?

3. **Product-Focused Content**
   - How do successful apps showcase features without being salesy?
   - What demo/tutorial formats work best?
   - How to handle "before/after" content sensitively?

4. **Community Building Content**
   - What engagement prompts work for this audience?
   - How to encourage user-generated content?
   - How to build a supportive community atmosphere?

**Research Questions:**
- What content-to-promotion ratio works best (80/20 rule)?
- How often should product be mentioned vs. pure value content?
- What CTAs convert best for app downloads?

---

### 1.4 Content Creation Requirements

**For Each Content Type, Document:**

#### Static Image Posts
- Ideal dimensions for each platform
- Design tools needed (Canva, Figma, Adobe Express)
- Brand consistency elements (colors, fonts, logo placement)
- Types: quotes, tips, statistics, announcements
- Stock photo resources for diverse family imagery
- Accessibility: alt text, contrast requirements

#### Carousel Posts
- Optimal number of slides
- Storytelling structure (hook → value → CTA)
- Design templates and tools
- Topics that work well as carousels

#### Short-Form Video (Reels/TikTok/Shorts)
- Equipment needed (phone, lighting, microphone)
- Editing apps (CapCut, InShot, Adobe Premiere Rush)
- Video formats: talking head, text overlay, screen recording, animation
- Hook strategies (first 3 seconds)
- Caption/subtitle requirements
- Music/sound selection for sensitive content
- Batch filming strategies

#### Long-Form Video (YouTube)
- Production quality requirements
- Script writing best practices
- Thumbnail design
- End screens and cards
- Content ideas: tutorials, interviews, Q&A

#### Stories
- Daily story strategy
- Interactive features (polls, questions, quizzes)
- Story highlights organization
- Behind-the-scenes content ideas

#### User-Generated Content
- How to solicit testimonials ethically
- Permission and release requirements
- Incentive programs (referrals, features)
- How to repurpose UGC across platforms

### 1.6 Sensitive Content Guidelines

**Critical Research:**
- How to discuss divorce/separation without negativity
- Avoiding content that could be used against users in court

## PART 3: Deliverables Expected from Research

After completing research, agents should produce:

1. **Platform Playbooks** - One document per platform with specific tactics
2. **Content Calendar Template** - 30-day sample calendar with content mix
3. **Content Creation SOPs** - Step-by-step guides for each content type
4. **Hashtag Research Doc** - Platform-specific hashtag lists
5. **Influencer Database** - List of potential partners with contact info
6. **Competitor Analysis** - What competitors do well/poorly
7. **Paid Ads Playbook** - Setup guides for each ad platform
8. **Metrics Dashboard Spec** - What to track and how
9. **Community Guidelines** - Response templates and escalation procedures
10. **Legal/Compliance Checklist** - Disclosures, disclaimers, permissions needed
