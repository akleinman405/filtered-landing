The Key Difference

OurFamilyWizard is designed to create a documented record of all communication. It's often court-ordered and focuses on accountability. However, you still see every hostile word the other person writes.

Filtered is designed to protect your mental health first. Our AI reads the hostility so you don't have to. You get the information you need (schedules, requests, emergencies) without the emotional attacks. Original messages are still stored for legal purposes.

Best for mental health: Filtered

When to Choose Filtered

  • The other person sends hostile, manipulative, or emotionally abusive messages
  • Reading their messages ruins your day or triggers anxiety
  • You want AI to help craft neutral, de-escalating responses
  • You want a simpler, more affordable solution
  • Your main need is mental health protection, not shared calendars

When to Choose OurFamilyWizard

  • It's court-ordered for your communication arrangement
  • You need shared expense tracking and reimbursement requests
  • Both people are willing to use the same app
  • You prioritize shared calendars over message filtering
