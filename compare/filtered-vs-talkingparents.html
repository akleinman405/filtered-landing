The Key Difference

TalkingParents focuses on creating an unalterable record of communication that can be used in court. It documents everything, but you still have to read every hostile message your co-parent sends. Their tagline is accountability.

Filtered focuses on protecting your mental health first. Our AI removes the hostility, manipulation, and emotional abuse from messages before you read them. You get the information you need without the pain. Original messages are still stored for legal purposes.

Best for emotional protection: Filtered

When to Choose Filtered

  • Your co-parent's messages trigger anxiety or ruin your day
  • You dread opening messages from your co-parent
  • You want AI to help craft neutral, de-escalating responses
  • You want protection from hostile communication, not just documentation of it
  • You want automatic task extraction from messages

When to Choose TalkingParents

  • Both parents agree to use the same platform
  • You need recorded phone calls (Accountable Calling feature)
  • Creating an unalterable legal record is your primary goal
  • You're okay reading hostile messages as long as they're documented

Can You Use Both?

Yes. Some parents use TalkingParents as their "official" court-ordered communication channel while using Filtered to screen messages before reading them on TalkingParents. You get the legal documentation benefits of one with the mental health protection of the other.
