# Filtered Social Media Strategy

**Created:** 2026-01-20
**Status:** Active

---

## Brand Voice & Positioning

### Tone of Voice
- **Empathetic, not clinical** - We understand the struggle
- **Warm but not preachy** - Ally, not authority
- **Acknowledge difficulty first** - Then offer solutions
- **Use "we/you" language** - Position as companions in the journey
- **Gentle humor when appropriate** - Normalize the struggle

### Unique Positioning
**"Filtered = Your co-parenting pause button"**
- First co-parenting app with emotional wellness integration
- Focus on communication improvement, not just documentation
- Target both parents equally (not mom-focused)
- Build community, not just a tool

## Platform Strategy

### Instagram (Primary)
- **Frequency:** 3-5 Reels + 1-2 Carousels + daily Stories weekly
- **Focus:** Educational carousels, relatable Reels, community Stories
- **Best times:** 7-9 AM, 7-9 PM (Tue-Thu)

### TikTok (Secondary)
- **Frequency:** 5-7 posts weekly
- **Focus:** Problem-solution videos, educational micro-series
- **Best times:** 5-9 PM (Mon-Thu), 8 PM Sunday

### Facebook (Tertiary)
- **Frequency:** 3-4 posts weekly
- **Focus:** Longer educational content, community building
- **Best times:** 11 AM Wed, 9 AM-12 PM Tue/Thu
