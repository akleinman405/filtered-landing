Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Filtered and how it protects your peace while keeping you connected.

How Filtered Works

How does the AI filtering work?

Our AI analyzes incoming messages and removes hostile, manipulative, or emotionally charged language while preserving the essential information. You see a summary of what matters: schedules, requests, and important updates about your children. You always have the option to view the original message if needed.

Will my co-parent know I'm using Filtered?

They'll text a different phone number (your Filtered number), but they won't see any indication that messages are being filtered. Your replies appear to come from your Filtered number. From their perspective, it's just a normal text conversation.

How is this different from blocking them?

Blocking cuts off communication entirely, which isn't an option when you need to co-parent. Filtered keeps you connected and informed while protecting your mental health. You stay in the loop about your children without the emotional toll of hostile messages.

Can I turn off filtering?

Yes. You have full control. You can view individual original messages anytime, or turn on "Show Original Messages" in settings to see everything unfiltered. It's your choice, and you can change it at any time.

Safety & Emergencies

What if there's an emergency about my child?

Our AI is specifically trained to detect urgent and emergency messages. These alert you immediately, even if the message contains hostile language. You'll never miss something important about your children's safety, health, or wellbeing.

What kinds of messages get flagged as urgent?

Messages about medical emergencies, safety concerns, immediate schedule changes, school issues requiring quick response, and anything involving your children's immediate wellbeing. The AI errs on the side of caution to ensure you're always informed when it matters.

Legal & Documentation

Can I use the original messages in court?

Yes. All original messages are stored securely and accessible anytime. You can view, export, and use them for legal documentation. We retain messages for 2 years specifically so you have records for custody proceedings, mediation, or court if needed.

How do I export messages for my lawyer?

In the app, go to Settings > Export Messages. You can export all conversations or specific date ranges as a PDF with timestamps, preserving both the filtered summaries and original messages.

Privacy & Security

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All messages are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption and in transit using TLS 1.3. We never sell your data to third parties. Your conversations are private and protected. See our Privacy Policy for complete details.

Who can see my messages?

Only you. Our AI processes messages automatically without human review. Support staff cannot access your message content. The only exception is if you explicitly share messages (like exporting for legal purposes) or if required by valid legal process.

How long are messages stored?

Messages are retained for 2 years from the date received. This gives you ample time for legal proceedings while ensuring we don't hold your data indefinitely. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

Pricing & Account

What happens after the free trial?

After 14 days, your subscription continues at $9/month. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends and won't be charged. No commitments, no contracts, no hidden fees.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period. We don't believe in trapping people in subscriptions.

What happens to my messages if I cancel?

Your message history remains accessible for 30 days after cancellation so you can export anything you need. After that, your data is deleted per our privacy policy.
