SMS Messaging Terms

Last Updated: December 29, 2025

Filtered uses SMS messaging to deliver filtered communications directly to your phone. This page explains how our SMS service works and your rights regarding text messages.

Opt-In and Consent

When you sign up for Filtered, you explicitly consent to receive SMS messages by:

  • Creating an account and agreeing to our Terms of Service
  • Completing the onboarding process and accepting a dedicated Filtered phone number
  • Providing your contact with your Filtered number for communication

By completing these steps, you provide your express written consent to receive SMS messages from Filtered at the phone number you provide.

Types of Messages

You may receive the following types of SMS messages from Filtered:

  • Filtered Messages: Forwarded messages from your contact that have been processed by our AI to remove hostile or harmful content
  • Emergency Alerts: Immediate notifications for time-sensitive matters or urgent schedule changes
  • Service Notifications: Important updates about your account, subscription, or service changes
  • Delivery Confirmations: Notifications that your messages have been sent successfully

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your contact's communication patterns. On average, users receive between 1-20 messages per week, but this can vary significantly. You have full control over notification settings within the Filtered app, allowing you to customize which messages trigger SMS alerts.

Quick Reference

Text STOP to opt out of all SMS messages
Text HELP for assistance and support information

How to Opt Out

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time using any of these methods:

  • Text STOP: Reply STOP to any message from your Filtered number to immediately stop receiving SMS messages
  • App Settings: Navigate to Settings > Notifications in the Filtered app and disable SMS notifications
  • Contact Support: Email us at alec@kleinmanventures.com to request SMS opt-out

After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message. You can continue to use the Filtered app to view messages, but will no longer receive SMS notifications.

Re-Subscribing

If you previously opted out and wish to receive SMS messages again:

  • Text START to your Filtered number, or
  • Enable SMS notifications in your app settings, or
  • Contact us at alec@kleinmanventures.com

Costs and Carrier Fees

Filtered does not charge per-message fees to users. However, standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply. Please check with your carrier regarding your SMS plan and any applicable charges.

Supported Carriers

Filtered SMS messages are supported by all major US carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and most regional carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your phone number and message data are protected in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes. Message content is encrypted and securely stored.

Getting Help

For any questions about SMS messaging or technical issues:

  • Text HELP to your Filtered number
  • Email us at alec@kleinmanventures.com
  • Visit our website at filteredmessaging.com

Changes to SMS Terms

We may update these SMS terms from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or through the app. Continued use of SMS features after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
